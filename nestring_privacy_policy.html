Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 16, 2026
At Nestring Foods, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you order food through our WhatsApp service, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1 Who We Are

Nestring Foods is a food delivery and takeaway service operating in Kenya. We use WhatsApp Business to receive orders, communicate with customers, and confirm deliveries.

We are the data controller for personal information you share with us when using our WhatsApp ordering service.

2 Information We Collect

When you interact with our WhatsApp ordering bot or our team, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and display name
  • Your delivery address or location (when shared)
  • Your order details — items ordered, quantities, and preferences
  • Your payment reference — M-Pesa transaction codes you send us
  • Your order history — past orders placed with us
  • Messages you send us through WhatsApp

We do not collect your M-Pesa PIN, full bank details, national ID, or any passwords.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your food orders
  • To confirm payment and update your order status
  • To contact you about your delivery — estimated time, driver details
  • To resolve complaints or issues with your order
  • To improve our service — understanding popular dishes and delivery areas
  • To send you order updates via WhatsApp (e.g. "Your order is on the way")

We do not use your information for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with any marketing companies.

4 WhatsApp & Meta

Our ordering service runs on WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you message us on WhatsApp:

  • Meta processes your messages in accordance with their own WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • We receive only the content of messages you send to our business number
  • We store order-related data in our secure systems to process your order
By messaging our WhatsApp number to place an order, you agree to WhatsApp's Terms of Service and consent to us processing your order information as described in this policy.

5 M-Pesa Payments

We accept payments via M-Pesa (Safaricom). When you pay:

  • You send money directly to our M-Pesa Till Number — we never handle your PIN
  • You may share your M-Pesa confirmation code with us to confirm payment
  • We store only the transaction reference code — not your full financial details
  • M-Pesa transactions are governed by Safaricom's Privacy Policy

6 Data Storage & Security

Your order data is stored securely using Google Firebase / Firestore, a cloud database with industry-standard encryption and security controls.

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access is restricted to authorised Nestring Foods staff only
  • We do not store payment card numbers or M-Pesa PINs
  • Order records are retained for up to 2 years for accounting and dispute resolution

7 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information only in these cases:

  • Delivery riders — your phone number and delivery area are shared with the rider assigned to your order
  • Legal requirements — if required by Kenyan law or a court order
  • Service providers — Firebase (Google) for data storage, Safaricom for payment processing

8 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data — we will delete your order history within 30 days of request, unless retention is required by law
  • Opt out of receiving WhatsApp notifications from us at any time by messaging us "STOP"

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9 Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our WhatsApp ordering service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
